About

Dr. Manoj Kumar Karnena is an Indian environmental scientist, researcher and educator. Since 2019 he has worked as a researcher in the Department of Environmental Science at GITAM Institute of Science, GITAM (Deemed to be) University, Visakhapatnam.

His research focuses on sustainable water and wastewater treatment, especially natural (green) coagulants as alternatives to chemical coagulants, along with nanocatalysis, storage technologies and membrane technology. He has published research papers, book chapters and books, and holds patents in his field.
