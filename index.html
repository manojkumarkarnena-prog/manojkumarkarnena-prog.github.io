About

Dr. Manoj Kumar Karnena is an Indian environmental scientist, researcher and educator. Since November 2023 he has been Assistant Professor of Water Treatment Technology in the School of Engineering at Yesbud University, Lusaka, Zambia, where he teaches courses, prepares study material and assesses students.

He earned his Ph.D. in Environmental Science (Water and Wastewater Treatment) from GITAM (Deemed to be) University, Visakhapatnam, in 2023, with doctoral research on natural coagulants as a nature-based solution for water and wastewater treatment. His work also covers nanocatalysis, membrane technology, hydrogen storage and GIS & remote sensing. He is a member of the Royal Society of Chemistry and qualified APSET-2020 in Environmental Science.
